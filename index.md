# NurseWiki

A free, open, and collaborative nursing reference.

NurseWiki is a structured knowledge base for nursing students and practising nurses. It covers foundations, clinical specialties, skills, drugs, Ghana-specific practice, and exam preparation.

## How to Use This Wiki

- **Browse by topic** using the sidebar on the left.
- **Search** using the search bar at the top.
- **Follow links** between pages to explore related concepts.

## Disclaimer

> **Educational use only.** This wiki is a study and reference tool. It is not a substitute for clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or the advice of a qualified healthcare provider. Always verify drug doses, procedures, and guidelines against current official sources before applying them in practice.
