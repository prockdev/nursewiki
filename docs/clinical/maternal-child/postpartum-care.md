# Postpartum Care

Care of the mother and newborn after birth.

## Maternal Assessment

- Bleeding
- Uterine tone
- Vital signs
- Pain
- Breast and nipple condition
- Emotional state

## Newborn Assessment

- Breathing
- Temperature
- Feeding
- Cord care
- Jaundice

## Warning Signs

- Heavy bleeding
- Fever
- Severe headache
- Difficulty breathing
