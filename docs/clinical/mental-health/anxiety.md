# Anxiety

A state of excessive worry or fear that interferes with daily functioning.

## Types

- Generalized anxiety
- Panic disorder
- Social anxiety
- Phobias

## Nursing Assessment

- Physical symptoms: palpitations, sweating, tremor
- Cognitive symptoms: worry, racing thoughts
- Behavioral symptoms: avoidance

## Nursing Interventions

- Calm, structured communication
- Breathing techniques
- Reduce environmental triggers
- Encourage professional support
