# Depression

A mood disorder characterized by persistent sadness, loss of interest, and functional impairment.

## Nursing Assessment

- Mood and affect
- Sleep and appetite
- Energy levels
- Suicidal ideation
- Social support

## Nursing Interventions

- Establish trust
- Encourage expression
- Monitor for self-harm
- Support medication adherence
- Involve family where appropriate
