# Diabetes Mellitus

A metabolic disorder characterized by elevated blood glucose due to insufficient insulin or insulin resistance.

## Types

- **Type 1**: Autoimmune destruction of insulin-producing cells.
- **Type 2**: Insulin resistance with relative insulin deficiency.
- **Gestational**: Occurs during pregnancy.

## Nursing Assessment

- Blood glucose monitoring
- Signs of hypo- and hyperglycemia
- Foot inspection
- HbA1c review

## Hypoglycemia vs Hyperglycemia

| Feature | Hypoglycemia | Hyperglycemia |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | Rapid | Gradual |
| Blood glucose | Low | High |
| Symptoms | Sweating, tremor, confusion | Thirst, polyuria, fatigue |
| Action | Fast-acting carbohydrate | Insulin, hydration, monitor |

## Patient Education

- Diet and exercise
- Self-monitoring
- Insulin technique
- Sick-day rules
