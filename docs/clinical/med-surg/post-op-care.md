# Post-Operative Care

Nursing care of the patient recovering from surgery.

## Priorities

1. Airway and breathing
2. Circulation
3. Pain management
4. Wound care
5. Early mobilization
6. Preventing complications

## Common Complications

- Atelectasis
- Deep vein thrombosis
- Infection
- Hemorrhage
- Paralytic ileus

## Nursing Actions

- Monitor vitals frequently
- Assess surgical site
- Encourage deep breathing and coughing
- Manage pain
- Document intake and output
