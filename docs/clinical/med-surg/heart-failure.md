# Heart Failure

A clinical syndrome where the heart cannot pump enough blood to meet the body's needs.

## Types

- **Left-sided failure**: Blood backs up into the lungs. Symptoms: dyspnea, crackles, orthopnea.
- **Right-sided failure**: Blood backs up into the systemic circulation. Symptoms: edema, JVD, weight gain.

## Nursing Assessment

- Daily weight
- Intake and output
- Lung sounds
- Edema
- Fatigue and activity tolerance

## Common Medications

- Diuretics
- ACE inhibitors
- Beta blockers
- Digoxin

## Patient Education

- Low-sodium diet
- Daily weighing
- Recognizing worsening symptoms
- Medication adherence
