# Microbiology

Microorganisms, infection, and the principles of asepsis in nursing practice.

## Overview

Microbiology explains how pathogens cause disease and how the body defends against them. For nurses, it underpins infection control, antibiotic stewardship, and patient education.

## Key Concepts

- **Pathogen**: A microorganism that causes disease.
- **Commensal**: A microorganism that lives on or in the body without causing harm.
- **Asepsis**: Practices that prevent contamination by pathogens.
- **Sterile technique**: Eliminating all microorganisms.
- **Clean technique**: Reducing the number of microorganisms.

## Common Pathogen Classes

| Class | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bacteria | *S. aureus*, *E. coli* | Treatable with antibiotics |
| Viruses | Influenza, HIV | Require antivirals or vaccines |
| Fungi | *Candida* | Often opportunistic |
| Parasites | *Plasmodium* | Common in tropical regions |

## Infection Control Basics

- Hand hygiene is the single most effective measure.
- Use personal protective equipment appropriately.
- Follow isolation precautions based on transmission route.
- Dispose of sharps safely.
