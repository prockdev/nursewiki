# Anatomy & Physiology

Foundational concepts of human structure and function relevant to nursing practice.

## Overview

Anatomy is the study of structure. Physiology is the study of function. Together they form the basis for understanding how the body works, how disease disrupts it, and how nursing interventions restore balance.

## Key Systems

| System | Primary Function | Nursing Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiovascular | Circulates blood, delivers oxygen | BP, pulse, perfusion, edema |
| Respiratory | Gas exchange | Rate, depth, oxygen saturation |
| Nervous | Coordinates body activities | Consciousness, pain, reflexes |
| Renal | Filters blood, balances fluids | Urine output, electrolytes |
| Endocrine | Hormonal regulation | Blood glucose, thyroid, stress response |

## Homeostasis

The body's tendency to maintain a stable internal environment. Most nursing care aims to support or restore homeostasis.

**Core variables kept in balance:**
- Temperature
- pH
- Blood glucose
- Fluid and electrolyte levels
- Blood pressure

## Clinical Application

When you assess a patient, you are checking how well their systems are maintaining homeostasis. Abnormal vitals are signals that one or more systems are under strain.
