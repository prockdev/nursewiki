# Pharmacology Basics

Core principles of how drugs work and how nurses safely administer them.

## Overview

Pharmacology is the study of drugs and their effects on the body. Nurses need to understand pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and the nursing responsibilities that come with every dose.

## Key Terms

- **Pharmacokinetics**: What the body does to the drug (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion).
- **Pharmacodynamics**: What the drug does to the body.
- **Half-life**: Time for the drug concentration to halve.
- **Therapeutic index**: Margin between effective and toxic doses.
- **Side effect**: Predictable, often unavoidable effect.
- **Adverse reaction**: Harmful, sometimes unpredictable effect.

## The Rights of Medication Administration

1. Right patient
2. Right drug
3. Right dose
4. Right route
5. Right time
6. Right documentation
7. Right reason
8. Right to refuse

## Nursing Responsibilities

- Verify the order.
- Check allergies.
- Know the drug's action and side effects.
- Monitor the patient's response.
- Educate the patient.
